Choose a well-water laboratory panel by the decision it must support, then confirm the laboratory's sampling instructions **before** collecting water. Annual source monitoring, a plumbing-lead investigation and verification of an installed filter can require different sampling points and procedures.

## Write the question on the order

| Your question | Discuss this sampling plan with the laboratory | What the result cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| What is in the untreated well water? | An appropriate untreated sampling point and local-risk panel | Conditions at every household faucet |
| Does household plumbing contribute contamination? | The specified tap and analyte-specific collection protocol | The condition of the well from a single downstream sample |
| Does the treatment address the target contaminant? | Matched untreated and treated samples under defined operating conditions | Future performance after media exhaustion or a source change |
| Has flooding or a spill affected the well? | Local health or environmental authority instructions | Clearance from a routine shopping-kit screen |

For example, a softener can change the hardness measured at a kitchen tap. That treated result is useful for checking softening but is the wrong input for sizing treatment of the raw source. Conversely, bypassing all treatment to collect a raw sample does not answer what someone is drinking from their normal faucet.

Do not change valves or open plumbing merely to obtain a sample. Have the laboratory and a qualified professional identify a suitable existing sampling point or arrange one.

## Choose analytes with local evidence

[EPA recommends annual private-well checks](https://www.epa.gov/privatewells/protect-your-homes-water) for total coliform bacteria, nitrate, pH and total dissolved solids, with additional tests based on local concerns and changed conditions. Ask the laboratory how its bacteria panel reports or follows up *E. coli*. Local health departments can identify relevant groundwater and land-use risks.

A new property with no reliable records may need a broader initial panel. Bring the well log, previous results, known treatment, nearby land-use concerns and any recent changes. Hardness, iron and manganese can be relevant to equipment selection; a standard mineral panel does not automatically include every health-related contaminant.

Ask for the **named analytes**, not simply a package called “complete.” Confirm that the laboratory is certified for the particular drinking-water tests ordered. A home strip or handheld TDS meter is not a substitute for that analysis when making a health-related treatment decision.

## Check the reporting limit before paying

Ask the laboratory which units and reporting limits it will use. “Not detected” means below the method's reporting limit, not necessarily absent. If that limit is too high to resolve the benchmark relevant to your decision, the result cannot give the answer you wanted.

Nitrate reported as nitrogen and nitrate reported as nitrate are different expressions. Do not compare a number in one form with a reference for the other. Likewise, milligrams per litre and micrograms per litre differ by a factor of 1,000. Ask the laboratory to interpret the result and applicable reference together rather than converting an unfamiliar report by guesswork.

Use the [results-reading guide](/guides/water-test-kit-results-explained-for-homeowners-what-they-mean-for-your-kitchen/) once the report arrives. A public-system regulatory action level is not a personal all-clear for an individual tap.

## Book collection and return as one job

Confirm bottles, preservatives, preparation, collection order, holding times and delivery arrangements for every analyte. Different tests may need separate bottles or protocols. Do not rinse supplied bottles or substitute a household jar unless the laboratory specifically directs it.

Arrange collection around laboratory acceptance and courier availability. A Friday collection is unsuitable if the required holding time expires before the laboratory can process it. Follow the lab's temperature and shipping instructions rather than applying one generic rule to all samples.

Keep the report with the sample location, date, treatment state and recent well work. That record makes a later comparison interpretable. An unexplained number from an unidentified tap does not.

## Let the result decide the next purchase

Discuss health-related exceedances or suspected contamination promptly with the local health department and follow its water-use advice. Do not wait for a filter delivery or assume boiling removes chemical contamination.

When equipment selection is appropriate, use the [well-water treatment checklist](/guides/well-water-filtration-checklist-for-first-time-homeowners-kitchen-setup/) and verify the exact model's claims through the [certification guide](/guides/nsf-water-filter-certification-42-53-58-401-claims/). This guide's contribution is the sampling-question matrix and ordering sequence. It is not a laboratory result, a diagnosis or a universal panel for every well.
