For a low-pressure RO installation, compare the **measured feed conditions with the exact system's operating limits**. There is no universal pressure band that makes every residential RO suitable. A booster pump also cannot repair an inadequate household supply or a blocked feed connection.

## Give the installer a pressure record

Record the measurement location, instrument, static pressure and pressure during normal demand. On a well, note the range across a normal pump cycle. A reading at a distant hose connection may differ from pressure at the RO feed after valves and prefilters.

| Observation | What to investigate before buying |
|---|---|
| Pressure is weak throughout the home | Supply, regulator, well system or distribution plumbing |
| Kitchen feed differs markedly from nearby supply | Local valve, tubing or restriction |
| House supply is adequate but RO production falls | Unit-specific prefilter, membrane, temperature or control troubleshooting |
| Storage-tank delivery is weak | Tank-side diagnosis under the manufacturer's procedure |
| Refrigerator alone struggles | Its inlet requirements and the RO branch design |

These observations narrow the investigation; they do not identify a failed component on their own. Have a competent installer make measurements where opening the plumbing is necessary.

## Distinguish the pressure devices

A compatible **booster pump** raises pressure within the RO feed arrangement. It needs the specified inlet conditions, controls and electrical installation. A **permeate pump** addresses the influence of tank backpressure in an appropriate tank system; it is not a general household pressure booster.

Ask the manufacturer which complete configuration is supported and whether the quoted performance requires accessories. Adding an arbitrary pump to a certified system can change the assembly and operating conditions behind its claims. Do not adjust a pressure switch, tank charge or regulator from a generic internet number.

## A useful acceptance brief

Write down the largest planned draw, refill requirement, cold-season supply conditions and any refrigerator connection. Ask the installer to demonstrate operation under the agreed conditions and explain alarms, shutoff behavior and maintenance. Rated gallons per day do not by themselves establish faucet delivery or full-tank refill time in your home.

If the feasible RO configuration cannot meet the need, revisit the treatment specification. A non-RO filter is an alternative only if it has the appropriate verified claim for the actual target; low pressure is not a reason to substitute an ineffective treatment.
