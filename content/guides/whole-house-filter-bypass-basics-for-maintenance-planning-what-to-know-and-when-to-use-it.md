A bypass routes water around treatment. It may keep downstream plumbing supplied, but **that water has not received the bypassed treatment**. A short service window does not automatically make untreated water suitable for drinking, cooking, bathing or other uses.

## Identify exactly what the bypass excludes

Trace the manufacturer's diagram before touching valves. A bypass might exclude one cartridge, a complete filter bank or a larger treatment train. A valve beside a tank is not enough to identify the route.

| Question | Required answer before service |
|---|---|
| Which stages are bypassed? | Named equipment and affected outlet branches |
| Why are they installed? | Nuisance treatment, equipment protection or a health-related duty |
| Is untreated use acceptable? | A documented use plan appropriate to the water concern |
| Can equipment be isolated and depressurized? | The exact manual's procedure, including trapped-pressure points |
| How is service restored? | Specified valve positions, flushing, sanitation and verification |

If the route or safe-use plan is unclear, arrange the service with the installer rather than guessing handle positions. Shutting off a supply does not by itself prove that every vessel is depressurized.

## Separate backwash from maintenance bypass

An automatic backwashing system uses a programmed hydraulic sequence. Do not place it in bypass simply because a backwash starts; that can interrupt the operation. Control-head repair and manual isolation are different tasks with their own instructions.

There is no universal seven-step valve sequence across integrated bypasses, three-valve loops, softeners and UV installations. Similarly, a generic seasonal valve-cycling schedule should not replace the installed manufacturer's service requirements.

## Use a return-to-service card

Record who performed the work, replaced parts, required sanitation or flush, final valve positions and leak check. Confirm that the intended treated outlets are back on the treated route. Where the water concern requires post-service testing or another release condition, complete it before resuming the affected uses.

This card prevents a common administrative failure: a mechanically finished cartridge change that leaves the house on bypass. It does not certify water quality by itself.

## Plan hardware while choosing the system

Ask whether the quote includes a manufacturer-supported bypass, isolation access and space to service wet components. More valves are not automatically better; they should make the route understandable and maintainable.
