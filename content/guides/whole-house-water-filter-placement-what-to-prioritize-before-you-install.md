Choose a whole-house filter location by drawing the treatment boundary and checking the exact equipment's service envelope. **A universal 12–18-inch clearance or 10 PSI loss limit is not a placement specification.** Different housings, tanks and controls need different space and operating conditions.

## Draw the branches before choosing a wall

Mark the source, existing controls, water heater, indoor branches, outdoor taps and any current treatment. Identify which uses should receive the proposed treatment. A filter after a branch cannot treat that branch; a unit on the wrong line can consume capacity on water the owner never intended to treat.

Do not change well pressure-control arrangements from a generic diagram. The relationship between pump, pressure tank, switch and restrictions must remain appropriate to the well system. Have the installer confirm the chosen connection point and the treatment sequence.

## Compare two candidate positions

| Constraint | Candidate A | Candidate B |
|---|---|---|
| Desired branches covered | Trace each branch | Trace each branch |
| Equipment and service envelope | Measure against manual | Measure against manual |
| Environmental limits | Check heat, freezing and exposure | Check heat, freezing and exposure |
| Drain or power where required | Confirm feasible route | Confirm feasible route |
| Isolation and access | Demonstrate reach and tool movement | Demonstrate reach and tool movement |
| Hydraulic effect | Assess added pipe/fitting and system losses | Assess added pipe/fitting and system losses |

Fill this in with measurements and installer findings, not a score. An essential requirement that fails cannot be outweighed by several conveniences.

## Measure the removal path

The installed body dimensions are only the starting point. A sump may need to drop clear; a tank control or media change may require overhead access. Include support, valve travel, tubing bends, tools and a place for wet parts. Use a cardboard outline of the documented envelope if it helps check the space, but do not substitute it for the manufacturer's clearance requirements.

A short pipe run may be attractive yet put the unit behind a fixed appliance. A slightly different route may improve service access but add hydraulic losses. Have both consequences evaluated before choosing.

## Confirm the treatment order

Sediment, carbon, softening and disinfection have different purposes and feed requirements. Neither “carbon first” nor “softener first” applies to every analysis and product. Ask for a labeled treatment diagram showing why each stage sits where it does.

[Pentair's housing dimensions and limits](https://www.pentair.eu/sites/default/files/2024-05/Technical_Sheet_BigBlue_Housings.pdf) illustrate the model-specific information to collect.
