Before opening a filter housing, verify its identity, condition, flow route and service procedure. **One unresolved critical condition is a stop**, even when the other checks look good. A scored “readiness” quiz should not let several favorable answers outweigh a cracked housing or an inability to release pressure.

## Use independent gates

| Gate | What you must establish | If unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Exact head/sump model and current service instructions | Obtain the correct manual |
| Flow direction | Supply and outlet match documented markings | Have the route checked |
| Condition | No damage or expired replacement requirement identified | Arrange replacement or assessment |
| Isolation | The assembly can be isolated as specified | Do not open it |
| Pressure release | The specified method has been followed and pressure safely relieved | Do not force the sump |
| Parts and access | Approved cartridge/seals and sufficient removal space | Prepare the correct service setup |

This is a pre-service check, not a certification that the housing is safe. Visual inspection cannot reveal every defect or replace a manufacturer-required replacement interval.

## Read markings rather than sediment patterns

Trace the supply toward the marked inlet and the outlet toward downstream plumbing. Cartridge flow paths vary, so use the actual assembly's diagram. Sediment in the bottom of a bowl does not prove that the ports are reversed. Nor does a clean bowl prove correct cartridge seating or effective treatment.

Opaque housings are not defective merely because you cannot see the media. Clouding, crazing, cracks or damaged threads on a component designed to be clear need assessment against its manual. Do not select a transparent replacement solely for visibility without checking its pressure, temperature and compatibility limits.

## Prepare the service envelope

Measure space for the sump to come away, the specified tool to move and the wet component to be supported. Keep a clean surface for seals and replacement parts, and protect the surrounding area from expected drainage. Do not let pipework carry a load the installation instructions require a bracket to support.

Use only approved seals, lubricants and cleaning procedures. An O-ring with the same apparent diameter may differ in section or material. Do not use extra wrench force to compensate for poor seating, an incorrect cartridge or damaged threads.

## Record the restart separately

Follow the unit's refill, flush and sanitation instructions, check for leaks and confirm the intended treatment route. If pressure measurements are part of maintenance, record the cartridge, flow condition and inlet/outlet readings after commissioning. That creates a meaningful comparison for the next service.
