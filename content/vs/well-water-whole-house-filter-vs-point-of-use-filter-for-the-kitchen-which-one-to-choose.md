For a private well, choose point-of-use or whole-house treatment from the **contaminant, relevant water uses and supported treatment method**. “Only the kitchen bothers me” is not enough to establish that untreated water is suitable everywhere else.

## Make an outlet-and-use map

List where drinking water, cooking water, ice and other relevant household uses come from. Include bathroom drinking, pet bowls, a second kitchen and visitors who may not know which tap is treated. Ask the local health authority or qualified treatment professional which uses matter for the identified concern; exposure is contaminant-specific.

| Proposed plan | What must be established |
|---|---|
| One treated drinking tap | All required consumption uses reach that tap and the method meets the target |
| Several point-of-use devices | Each necessary outlet is covered and every cartridge is maintained |
| Point-of-entry treatment | The complete system meets the target at household demand |
| Entry treatment plus a tap stage | Each stage has a distinct justified purpose |

The map is a planning aid, not a reason to ignore other water uses or a health advisory.

## Start with an appropriate well assessment

Use a certified laboratory and local risk advice to choose the panel. Identify whether samples represent untreated source water or water after existing equipment. Follow instructions on water use if a result or event raises a health concern. [EPA's well guidance](https://www.epa.gov/privatewells/protect-your-homes-water) explains routine testing and circumstances that call for additional attention.

After a treatment plan is installed, verify the relevant result at the specified point and maintain the equipment. A clear glass, pleasant taste or a lower TDS number cannot confirm every treatment claim.

## The two named products have limited roles

The [iSpring US31](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07CHXVRS5?tag=betterwaterguide-20) is a sediment-and-carbon under-sink example. It is not a complete solution for an unsafe well. Use it only when its intended feed-water conditions and exact claims fit the established need.

The [iSpring WGB32BM](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B01FI3BLYM?tag=betterwaterguide-20) adds a specific iron/manganese cartridge to sediment and carbon stages. It is not disinfection. Its [review](/reviews/ispring-whole-house-water-filter-is-it-worth-the-cost-for-cleaner-water-buyer-guide/) explains the different capacity basis for that cartridge.

Neither example becomes suitable for nitrate or microbial treatment simply by choosing the larger format. Select a different supported method when the target requires it.

## Test the ownership plan

For point-of-use, identify who replaces each element and how everyone finds the correct tap. For point-of-entry, identify service interruption, permitted untreated use, alarms and the responsible service provider. A single centralized system can simplify oversight but also makes its failure relevant to every downstream outlet.

Choose the arrangement that covers the documented uses and can be maintained consistently.
